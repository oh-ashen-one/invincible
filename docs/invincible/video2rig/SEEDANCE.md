## Rule reconciliation (recorded, not to be re-litigated)

- The hero is human, so the shared brain's source-first animation rule (Mixamo / Fab / SimReady first) normally applies. The owner's
  instruction of 2026-10-30 is the explicit per-task exception for this round: AI video of our own rig through video2rig.
- Provider: Seedance 2.5 on Higgsfield (`generate_video`, model `seedance_2_5`, mode `omni_reference`, `start_image` = our start
  picture, 720p, 16:9, 5 s, generate_audio ON (with audio off 4 of 5 Hulk outputs were refused after rendering), literal prompt with the
  preset recommendation declined; 35 credits per take). Balance at start: 267.64 credits (7 takes). One take at a time.
- Ship rule: a take is judged on its own look FIRST, beside the old clip on a close-up (weight, anticipation, follow-through,
  fluidity, nothing stiff). A take that is not visibly better is not fitted, whatever `check` says. In-game blind A/B ships only
  "visibly better", never "tie". Judge on close-ups, never on 960x540 wide shots. If the first blind round is flat: stop, report.
- GPU: cap three renderer-bearing clients machine-wide; everything through gpu_slot; no launch at util >= 90 %; today the readers of
  Markhor (until ~16:15 ET) then Dragon (~3 h) hold the GPU, so reader training, pose renders and the Unreal rebuild queue behind
  them and are announced to midir-60 / midir-5b / midir-dd before launch. Fits run on the CPU (`V2R_DEVICE=cpu`).

Why LandHero and Takeoff failed the first check: `video2rig check` aligns each panel camera by fitting the project's START POSE to frame 0 (creature.py `_load`, `clip.align`). In the `hero` project the start pose is the hover pose, so for clips made from the dive and the standing pictures the cameras were aligned to the wrong silhouette (frame-0 outline 0.40 / 0.42; a clip from its own start picture reads 0.85+). Misaligned cameras inflate the panel disagreement. Fix (2026-10-31): sibling projects that share the trained reader and clips but carry their own rig, start pictures and floor:

| project | rig (start pose) | start pictures | floor (armature units) | model for import |

| `Local/video2rig/hero-stand` (neutral path `Local/v2r-stand`) | `rig-stand` | `start-stand/` | -88.785 | `model_stand.blend` |

Root heights: every model is the same armature moved up or down as an object (`build_info*.json` `armature_location`), so a fit's root keys are in that model's armature space and its floor value is the floor in that space. The game's floor for a standing pose is -88.785 (the stand model). A LandHero fit in `hero-dive` lands on -153.556, 0.65 m lower: before export its root needs +64.77 units in z (or the dive model rebuilt on the game floor), or the landing sinks into the ground. Checked before anything ships.

Close-up placement bias found (2026-10-31 03:00): a fit GLB keeps its model's armature OBJECT offset (Hover 1.83 m, dive 1.54 m) while the game GLB's armature sits at the origin, and `cpu_closeup_hero.py` put the floor at min(frame 0's lowest point, 0) and framed frame 0. So the Hover rounds above drew the NEW hover 0.73 m over its floor and the OLD one with its feet on the floor: the critics' "reads as floating vs standing" was partly placement, not motion. The armature-space motion is right: the new LandHero's standing end matches the old one's to 0.01 units (pelvis 13.48, left foot -77.06). Fix: `cpu_closeup_hero.py --arm-origin` (both), `--frame-all` on the old, `--frame-from <old>_info.json` on the new (same target, body height, facing and floor). The Hover A/B is redone on matched renders; the two rounds above do not count toward shipping. The first LandHero close-up ("body under the floor") was the same artefact plus frame-0 framing of a clip that starts 1.2 m up.
