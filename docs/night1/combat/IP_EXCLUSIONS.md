# P5 combat: IP exclusions

> Homage fan game. Not an official Marvel, Sony or Insomniac game; no affiliation.

Round 01 uses no city texture atlas (`ts_ads.webp`, `ts_signs.webp`, `city_signart.webp`, `vehicles_atlas2.webp`) and no copied
names, logos or art. Combat_Street is procedural (`Scripts/build_combat.py`: HLSL facade / road / sidewalk materials, engine basic
shapes for cars, lamps, hydrants). Combat FX are engine basic shapes with generated materials. Characters are P2's own content
(owner's Tripo people, generic weapons) and the hero GLB already in the repo; the hero suit design remains P2's open brand flag.
