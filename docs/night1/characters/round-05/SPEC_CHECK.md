## What changed in the numbers since round 04

- The armpit webs (skin / cloth spikes between the swinging arm and the torso) are gone: hull triangles that bridge two limb groups are dropped (`crowd_tracking_4k.jpg`, black-tee walker: clean torso and armpit; round 04 and the round-05 WIP build had black / orange shards there).
- Holes in the vest chest / pocket cut-outs are filled by hull patches; long coats hang as one piece in every offline pose and in the Blender re-import of the FBX (see problem 1 for the engine).
- The crowd now has walkers 4.5 m from the camera (round 04: nobody nearer than 0.29 H), which exposes far more surface at 4K than round 04 did: compare defects per person, not the raw counts.

## Known problems (builder, native 4K unless noted)

1. **Hijabi trench coat in the engine** (`crowd_tracking.mp4` 4.5-7 s, `crowd_tracking_4k.jpg` right walker): a large triangular flap of the near front panel juts forward at knee height with a dark region behind it, and the hem is cut into slits. It is **pixel-identical between the round-05 WIP build (old weights) and the final build (skirt rig, expanded triangles)**, so the weights I changed do not reach that geometry in Unreal, although `crack_render.py` (30 phases of walkF) and a Blender re-import of the exported FBX (10 phases) show a coherent coat with the new weights, and the raw pack weights reproduce tears of that kind. Unresolved. Leads: every citizen FBX logs `BindPose Matrix generation acquired 2 different Matrices from FbxCluster vs FbxPose` on import; a nullrhi commandlet export of the imported mesh crashed (`SkinnedMeshComponent.cpp:4987`, one crash, not retried); next test = a debug FBX with an exaggerated weighting (e.g. all coat vertices 100 % on `head`) and see whether the engine moves them.
2. **3 mm triangle expansion** closes hairline gaps but leaves tiny thorns along trouser edges and sharper fingertips (near-lane hands in `crowd_tracking_4k.jpg`); the key-hole count improved only 13 %.
3. **`cracks.py` counts hair, face and hand highlights** (19 of 37 in the tracking still); key-hole counts are noisy between stills (director clock jitter), only totals over several stills mean anything.
4. Coat / hoodie pockets show black rectangular patches on the near-lane lumberjack vest (`crowd_key_c_4k.jpg`): hull interior in shadow, not see-through.
5. Fight: enemies hold their guard in a ring and swing / punch every 4-6 s; in the 8 s 3/4 clip the hero throws one high kick (1.3 s) and reels from a bat swing (6.3 s), otherwise it holds its guard, so the fight reads as a standoff with beats, not continuous combat; the three fight movies were shot with motion blur on.
6. Hero: lens has no bright sky highlight; the suit design (white spider on the chest and back, blue legs with red stripes) is still the open brand flag (owner decision); hero height in the chase camera is 0.42, below the standing-hero band.
7. Face close-ups: `Char_Lineup` shows other characters behind the subject in `tee_face_4k.jpg` / `beard_face_4k.jpg`.
8. Performance is not measured (shared GPU, contaminated); no claim about 60 fps for the crowd with its 12-14k-triangle hulls per citizen.
