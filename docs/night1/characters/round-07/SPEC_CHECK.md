**How the key works now, and why the numbers changed so much.** Round 05/06 painted the street, facades and windows an unlit green and removed fog and sky: every garment then caught the green bounce light (and the real-time sky light was captured from the green scene), so `G>R+40` flagged whole trouser legs, coats and the white tee (235k of 795k person pixels in round 06) although nothing was see-through. `Char_CrowdKey` is now the normal crowd map (same sun, sky, fog, street = the same lighting as the colour captures); the citizens write custom-depth stencil, and a before-bloom post-process material with the material's own stencil test paints everything that is not a citizen one flat key green and never touches a citizen pixel. So a green pixel inside a citizen silhouette is either see-through or a real green/teal garment colour, and a garment can no longer be tinted. Key level after tonemapping (8,235,3) (round 06: (0,217,0), it varied). The four stills are PNG (lossless; 4:2:0 JPEG bleeds the key into edge pixels).

**What is left in the enclosed-gap census (`key_check_r7.py`, hand-classified on the 3x montages `evidence/keycheck/*_components.jpg`):** `_a` 9 components / 4,287 px, `_c` 12 / 1,563 px, `_tracking` 10 / 602 px, `_wide` 15 / 483 px. All 46 are air: between the two legs of one stride (the largest, 3,109 px in `_a`), between two overlapping walkers, between a sleeve and a bag strap, between a hand and a hem, between fingers. **None is a hole through a coat, sleeve, torso or trouser panel.**

**3D separation is the pass; screen-space contact is reported, not claimed.** The brief's "no two walker masks overlapping" is met as no two bodies intersecting in the world (table row 2). On the screen a two-way flow filmed side-on cannot be free of depth occlusion: a near person covers part of a far one. From the per-walker id movie (`tools/ue_char/crowd/id_movie.sh`, frame-exact with `crowd_tracking.mp4`, 444 frames = 0.6-8 s): 9-12 walkers visible (median 11), **median 4 pairs of silhouettes touching within 2 px per frame (3 within 1 px); frames with no touching pair: 1 of 444.** Only the near-lane walkers were kept from touching each other (the near lane walks one way, 0 near-near contacts in the layout search); the two stills `_a` / `_c` are frames 194 / 261 of the 4K key movie, picked where no near-lane silhouette touches a mid-lane walker (`_c`: one mild contact, the dapper elder's hat behind the kurta man's shoulder, 4.4 m behind him). Raising the mid-lane spacing to cut the contacts further costs density (layout search with heavy overlap weight: 8.5 people in frame, 1.9 touching pairs, still 95 % of frames with a contact).

**Root cause of the "ankle-cuff gaps": the pavement.** `build_characters.py` `new_stage` makes the south sidewalk a 30 cm thick box centred on z = 0 (top face z = +15 cm) and every crowd round spawned the mid lane at z = 0: shoes and ankles stood 15 cm inside the pavement. In the round-06 key images the green floor plane sliced through the foot at the cuff / collar height (the "sliver between the trouser cuff and the shoe collar", and the larger wedge behind it). The first round-07 stencil key exposed it as pale foot-shaped blobs (custom stencil ignores scene occlusion: the buried foot keeps its stencil but shows the floor colour). The mid lane now spawns at z = 15 cm. The offline mesh checks found no cuff/shoe opening for that citizen (`ankle_gap.py`: no cross-shell pairs within 2 cm for the dapper elder), which is how the real cause was found. A skin gradient across the ankle joint (`weights_r6.py ankle_blend`) was added anyway: cross-shell openings above 5 mm in the walk clips 358 -> 100 vertex pairs over the 18 citizens (`evidence/ankle_gap_round0*_weights.txt`); offline CH18 gate over the 18 citizens (`eval_r6.py`): crack components 251 / 12,605 px (round 06: 244 / 13,362), triangles growing > 5 cm 7 (round 06: 13), > 10 cm 0, worst edge growth 5.6 cm (unchanged).

## Known problems (builder)

1. **Screen-space contact between walkers** (above): median 4 touching silhouette pairs per frame in the tracking shot. A literal "no two masks ever touch" is impossible with a side-on two-way flow at this density; the 3D intersection is 0.
2. The critic's secondary items are untouched: original suit design (IP flag, owner decision), one closed lens rim, fight hit reactions / knockdowns, masks drape, hood-interior shadow wedge, arms hanging out ~30 deg, the rear foot of the coat walker flicking to knee height in the stride (visible in `jeans-leg_3x.jpg`), crowd density below 16 in the tracking shot.
3. Citizens are low-poly (6 k triangles): fingers are faceted wedges, arms 10-12-sided tubes; the silhouette shows it at 4K.
4. Performance not measured (shared GPU, contaminated slots).
5. The mid-lane pavement fix changes every crowd capture of earlier rounds (their walkers were 15 cm into the ground).
