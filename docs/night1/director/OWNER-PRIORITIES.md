# Owner priorities (2026-10-01, after the first morning playtest)

**North star (owner's words, corrected 2026-10-01):** "What I'm interested in building isn't an entire game. I want this to be the most beautiful 4K ultra-realistic high-quality playable version of [the hero] swinging around doing tricks and exploring the highest-quality version of Manhattan. That's what I care about."

So: a traversal-and-exploration experience, not a full game with missions and systems. Every round should make swinging, tricks, the camera, or Manhattan itself look and feel better at 4K. Game systems are built only where they serve that (HUD, save games, settings).

## FIRST PASS: do these first, everything else waits (owner, 2026-10-01)
"I want to get the first pass done first. The rest of the stuff is kind of secondary. The most visually beautiful swinging-around concepts are most important."
1. **The swinging around:** web-swing feel and camera at 4K.
2. **The beautiful sky.**
3. **Beautiful terrain:** ground, parks, shorelines, what you see from above.
4. **The entire beautiful, well-done 4K Manhattan:** the whole island at full detail.
5. **The water effects we imported:** rivers and harbour.
6. **Hero skins and tricks:** different skins (all ORIGINAL designs, never official suits or emblems) and many tricks, with really high-quality animations when he flips around in the air.

Only after the first pass: HUD, save games, landmarks beyond the city build, boats, rain, breakables, then the rest.

## Important: build these (second pass, after the first pass above)
- **The whole physical Manhattan at full detail.** The playable detailed area today is a 3x3 block of 256 m tiles (~770 m square, about 4 % of the browser island); the rest is low-detail facade masses. Expanding it to the full island at full quality is the largest job.
- **Landmarks** (Times Square, Grand Central, bridges, the rest of the browser game's landmarks).
- **Boats** on the rivers.
- **Rain** (weather).
- **Breakable objects** (destruction).
- **HUD.**
- **Save games.**
- Plus the existing quality pieces, which serve the north star directly: traversal + gymnast flips, camera, look and lighting, 4K performance, characters, city life.

## Not important: do not spend rounds on these
- Crimes and street events.
- Collectibles.
- Skills and progression.
- **Photo mode: never build it.**
- **Audio: the owner adds audio themselves. Do not build audio.**
- Small background details (pigeons and other fauna, tiny props) are low priority.

## Not mentioned yet (deferred; ask the owner before investing)
- Fast travel and towers, the in-game map and markers, combat inside the city (combat currently lives on a separate test street).

## Owner-requested features already in progress
- Settings menu (sensitivity, invert Y, FOV, camera shake, graphics quality defaulting to max), PS5 controller support (no button-map screen), launch at max settings on the 3440x1440 ultrawide.
