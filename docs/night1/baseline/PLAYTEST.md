# Browser game playtest: bugs, console, asset checks, gaps ("before")

> Homage fan game. Not an official Marvel, Sony or Insomniac game; no affiliation. See `DISCLAIMER.md`.
> "Reference" images named below live in the private repo `~/spiderman-learnings/refs/` and are cited by path only.

Build under test: branch `night1/browser-baseline` (game code identical to `Opus-5.5-Loop-Night-1` at `da1a83f`), dev server on port 5201, Chrome 154 headless (`--headless=new`, Metal ANGLE, Apple M3 Ultra), played by an agent with real CDP key / mouse events. Clips are 1920x1080, frame-stepped 60 fps of game time (see `README.md`); stills are 3840x2160. Timestamps are game seconds from the start of the clip. Evidence lives beside this file: `clips/`, `stills/`, `stills/skins/`, `stills/bugs/`, `logs/`.

Everything in the game ran: 8 clips (swing chain, release + trick + dive + zip, wall run to a 235 m roof, street sprint, street fight, river pass, 8 suits, crowd / animals) plus a car-block clip; no crash, no hang, no white / black screen, no exception other than the harness-caused pointer-lock error. The problems below are polish, correctness and fidelity problems of a working game.

## 4. Biggest visible quality gaps vs Marvel's Spider-Man 2

Compared side by side on the same view. Reference = `~/spiderman-learnings/refs/<path>`; ours = this folder.

1. **Lighting, GI and atmosphere (widest gap).** Reference `characters/hero-idle-street-og__og_0000.jpg` and `traversal/swing-canyon-chase-high__nm_0141.jpg`: warm low sun, coloured bounce into the shadowed facades, lit interiors in varied warm / cool tones, haze layers, wet reflective asphalt. Ours `stills/street.jpg`, `stills/swing_tod_sunset.jpg`: hard sun and flat cool fill, shadowed sides are dull grey-brown with one repeated window texture, "sunset" is not golden, and night collapses to black (bug 8).
2. **Facade and street-level detail.** Reference `streets/street-sidewalk-pedestrians__dn_0721.jpg`: planters, hedges, bagged trash, stoops, fire escapes, awnings with depth, cracked and wet pavement. Ours `stills/street.jpg`, `clips/crowd.mp4`: flat storefront glass with fake dark reflections, moire shutters (bug 7), repeated brick / window tiles, sparse clutter (a few red news boxes, cones), blocky glass-tower mullions (`stills/swingBack.jpg`, left).
3. **City life fidelity.** Reference sidewalk: dense, varied, individually animated people who walk, talk and avoid each other. Ours: crowd clones in one waving loop and overlapping (bug 6), stiff dogs, visible seams / slits on clothing (bug 11), only a handful of readable animals.
4. **Camera and traversal readability.** Reference chase camera stays clear of foliage and buildings, frames the hero large with sky above (`traversal/swing-avenue-chase__nm_0642.jpg`). Ours: camera can fall to top-down under trees (bug 1), sits inside the hero (bug 2), presses against facades in canyons (`clips/swingChain.mp4` @ 3.4 s), and the hero cannot cross a parked car (bug 4). Swing speeds in the chain clip reach 55-60 m/s (no reference speed was measured).
5. **Character and combat finish.** Reference `combat/group-fight-nm__nm_0223.jpg`: varied enemy silhouettes, ragdoll knockdowns, large contact FX, cinematic framing. Ours `clips/fight.mp4`: every beat fires and reads (4-hit combo, dodge, web shooter, launcher, web strike, finisher; player hp 100 to 19 in 25 s), knocked-down thugs lie flat and the hits show yellow impact bursts, but reactions are short and stiff, the FX are small, and the framing is a plain mid-distance chase view. The Advanced suit is good in a close-up (`stills/suit.jpg`: fabric weave, raised web lines); the five AI-logo skins are flatter and carry the crack artifacts of bug 11 (`stills/skins/*_head.jpg`).

Also behind the reference but lower priority: water and shoreline are faceted (`stills/pond.jpg`, `stills/parkHigh.jpg` shows a stair-stepped pond edge, ferries and bridges are blocky in `stills/eastRiver.jpg`), underwater is a uniform teal with no particulate (`stills/underwater.jpg`), and far LODs pop in `stills/gulls.jpg`.
